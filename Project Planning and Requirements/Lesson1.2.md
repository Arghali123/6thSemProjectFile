## 1. What is a problem statement?

A problem statement explains:

* What problem exists?

* Who experiences the problem?

* Why does the problem matter?

* Why are existing solutions insufficient?

* How will our proposed system address it?

In simple words, a problem statement explains why our project needs to exist.

### Example

Suppose a student wants to become a software developer but doesn't know:

* Which skills are required.

* Which skills they already possess.

* Which skills they are missing.

* What they should learn next.

* Which career path matches their interests and abilities.

Our platform aims to address these difficulties.

## 4. Proposal-ready Problem Statement

The following draft can be included in your 6th-semester project proposal.

Problem Statement

## Problem Statement

Choosing a suitable career path and developing the skills required for professional growth are significant challenges faced by many students and job seekers. Individuals often lack clear information about the skills required for different career opportunities and may find it difficult to evaluate their existing abilities against industry expectations.

Traditional career guidance approaches and general learning platforms may not provide an integrated, personalized process for career exploration, skill assessment, skill-gap identification, and learning progress tracking. As a result, users may struggle to determine which skills they should improve and what learning activities they should prioritize.

Furthermore, extracting relevant skills from resumes and connecting them with suitable career opportunities can require manual effort. A unified platform that combines skill assessment, career matching, AI-assisted resume analysis, and personalized learning recommendations could help address these challenges.

Therefore, the proposed AI Skill & Career Management Platform aims to provide an intelligent, centralized solution that enables users to assess their skills, explore compatible career paths, identify skill gaps, receive personalized learning recommendations, and monitor their professional development through an interactive dashboard.
