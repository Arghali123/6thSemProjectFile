# Lesson 1.4 — Existing System & Limitations

## 1. What is an existing system?

An existing system refers to the applications, platforms, or methods that people currently use to solve a problem similar to the one our project addresses.

Before developing our platform, we should understand:

* What solutions are already available?

* What features do they provide?

* How do users benefit from them?

* What limitations remain?

* How can our proposed system address a specific gap?

Important: We should not claim that existing applications are ineffective. Instead, we identify the particular functionality our project intends to integrate or improve.

## 4. Limitations and challenges of existing approaches

For our proposal, we can identify the following potential limitations in fragmented career-development workflows.

### Limitation 1: Fragmented information

Users may need to switch between separate platforms for career information, skill assessment, learning, and progress tracking.

### Limitation 2: Difficulty identifying skill gaps

A user may know a career goal but still need help comparing their current skills against the skills required for that career.

### Limitation 3: Generic learning suggestions

General course catalogs provide many options, but users may need recommendations connected directly to their specific skill gaps.

### Limitation 4: Limited integration of career and learning progress

Career exploration, skill development, and learning progress may be managed separately rather than through one unified workflow.

### Limitation 5: Manual resume skill identification

Users may need to identify and organize their skills manually before using them for career exploration.

These are proposed problem areas for our project. We should validate specific claims about individual platforms through their official feature documentation before including them as factual comparisons.

Existing System and Its Limitations

## Existing System and Its Limitations

Several existing platforms provide services related to online learning, career exploration, skill assessment, and professional development. Platforms such as Coursera and LinkedIn Learning provide educational resources and professional learning opportunities, while O*NET Online provides occupational information, including skills and job-related requirements.

Although these platforms offer useful services, users may need to access multiple systems to manage different aspects of their career development. For example, career exploration, skill evaluation, learning resource selection, and progress monitoring may occur through separate processes.

The following challenges are considered in the context of the proposed project:

1. **Fragmented Career Development:** Users may need to use multiple platforms to access career information, learning resources, and skill assessment services.

2. **Skill-Gap Identification:** Comparing an individual's existing skills with the requirements of a selected career may require additional manual effort.

3. **Personalized Learning:** General learning resources may not always be organized according to an individual's assessed skill gaps and career objectives.

4. **Integrated Progress Monitoring:** Users may lack a single interface that combines assessment results, career compatibility, skill development, and learning progress.

5. **Resume Skill Extraction:** Identifying relevant skills from resumes and organizing them for career exploration may require manual work.

These challenges highlight the need for an integrated platform that combines skill assessment, career matching, skill-gap analysis, AI-assisted resume analysis, personalized learning recommendations, and progress visualization.

The proposed AI Skill & Career Management Platform aims to address these requirements through a centralized web-based system supported by Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and a career-matching algorithm.
