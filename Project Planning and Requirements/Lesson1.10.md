## Project Scope

The AI Skill & Career Management Platform is a web-based application intended to help students and job seekers identify their skills, explore suitable career paths, analyze skill gaps, and receive personalized learning recommendations.

### In-Scope

The project will include the following functionalities:

1. User registration, authentication, and role-based access control.

2. Student profile creation and management.

3. Skill management and skill assessment.

4. Career profile management with required skills.

5. A career-matching algorithm to identify suitable career paths based on user skills and career requirements.

6. Skill-gap analysis to identify skills that need improvement.

7. Learning resource recommendations based on identified skill gaps.

8. AI-assisted resume analysis for extracting relevant skills.

9. A dashboard for displaying career matches, assessment results, skill gaps, and learning progress.

10. An administrative module for managing users, career profiles, skills, assessment questions, and learning resources.

11. PostgreSQL database integration and secure data management.

12. Docker-based deployment and basic CI/CD implementation, subject to project time and infrastructure availability.

### Out-of-Scope

The following functionalities are excluded from the current implementation:

1. Development of native Android and iOS applications.

2. A complete job recruitment and employer hiring platform.

3. Guaranteed employment or job placement services.

4. Integration with every external job portal.

5. Advanced conversational AI career coaching.

6. Enterprise-scale infrastructure and deployment.

7. Complex subscription and billing management.

An online payment gateway may be included as an optional enhancement if sufficient development time and suitable integration facilities are available.

### Project Boundary

The system will focus on career exploration, skill assessment, career compatibility analysis, skill-gap identification, and learning recommendations. It will provide decision-support information rather than guarantee career outcomes or employment.

The implementation will prioritize a functional minimum viable product using React, Spring Boot, and PostgreSQL, with AI-assisted features integrated where technically and practically feasible.

Advanced functionalities may be considered for future enhancement without affecting the completion of the core project.

## 6. Practical Example: Scope Creep

Suppose you and Niraj initially decide to develop career matching and skill-gap analysis.

Later, you consider adding:

* A complete job portal

* Video calling with career experts

* A mobile application

* An AI interview simulator

These additions may increase development time significantly.

This is called scope creep — the uncontrolled expansion of project requirements.

To prevent it, discuss every new feature with your teammate and supervisor, then decide whether it belongs in the MVP or future enhancements.
